fix(venv): expose the interpreter under its own name; clear 12 stale test markers

test_venv's helper derives the venv python path from
sys._base_executable's basename (envpy() -> Scripts\rustpython.exe), but the
venv only contained python.exe/pythonw.exe copies, so every venv subprocess
test failed with FileNotFoundError and was masked by expectedFailure
markers.

- Lib/venv/__init__.py (Windows setup_python): also copy the interpreter
  under its own name (e.g. rustpython.exe) into Scripts. The C-API relay
  (rustpythonapi.dll) is name-independent, so this exe loads extensions
  exactly like python.exe.
- Lib/test/test_venv.py: remove 12 @unittest.expectedFailure markers that
  the venv/rely work now makes pass:
  test_prefixes, test_sysconfig, test_executable, test_multiprocessing,
  test_multiprocessing_recursion, test_defaults_with_str_path,
  test_defaults_with_pathlike, test_special_chars_windows,
  test_unicode_in_batch_file, test_upgrade, test_no_pip_by_default,
  test_explicit_no_pip.

test_venv now runs 40 tests green (12 posix/symlink-only skips remain), up
from 40 with 12 stale expected-failures; regression: test_importlib 1440,
test_ctypes 328, test_sysconfig 37, pyd smoke all pass.
