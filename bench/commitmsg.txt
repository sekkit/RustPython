fix(ctypes): correct cast() for callbacks and byref results

- PyCFuncPtr was cast to its buffer address (causing DEP crashes when
  invoked); cast it to the stored function pointer instead (CPython
  casts CFuncPtr to the pointer value in its buffer)
- cast(byref(x)) raised TypeError; extract the pointer from CArgObject
  like CPython

Verified: cast(byref(x)) equals addressof(x); cast(callback) yields the
function pointer; both match CPython. test_ctypes passes (328 run,
58 skipped).

With this, all four ctypes defects from the C-extension route report
(bitfields, struct-array elements, callbacks as arguments, cast) are
fixed.
