fix(ctypes): implement bitfield read-modify-write semantics

Bitfield fields (e.g. ("a", ctypes.c_int, 3)) were stored and read as
plain integers, so adjacent bitfields overwrote each other (setting
a=5,b=3 then reading a returned 3). CPython's b_get/b_set (cfield.c)
mask and shift the storage unit.

- descr_get: extract the bitfield with unsigned shift + mask, then
  sign-extend for signed ctypes types (b/h/i/l/q/n) — matches CPython
  exactly (verified: c_int bitfield 1/3/4 layout reads back identically,
  including 1-bit signed -1 semantics)
- descr_set: read-modify-write the storage unit (clear field bits, OR in
  masked value) instead of overwriting the whole unit

Verified: signed/unsigned bitfield readback and overwrite match CPython;
test_ctypes passes (328 run, 58 skipped).
